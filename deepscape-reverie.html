01-Deepscape Reverie

View YouTube ↗ Open Fast 3D Scene ↗ Open Full Experience ↗ View GitHub

This design centers on the individual memories of Shenjing Village, integrating residents' oral histories and spatial memories through digital technology. By mapping the relationships between "space—events—emotions" via knowledge graphs, it abstracts these into spatial prototypes guided by light and shadow. Combining dreamcore-style imagery with scripted game chapters, the design constructs an online exhibition narrative experience that encourages user participation in memory interaction. This achieves the digital translation of individual memories into collective memory, exploring new pathways for digital heritage presentation.
